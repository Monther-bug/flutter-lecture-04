Widget 4 · Container

Container: a box for your widgets

margin: space outside
color: the box color
padding: space inside
child
width / heightThe size of the box
color:The box's background color
padding:Space inside the box, around the child

Like a gift box: padding is the soft paper inside, margin is the space between boxes.
Mobile App Development · Lecture 04

Padding and margin take an EdgeInsets: EdgeInsets.all(16) means 16 on every side. EdgeInsets.symmetric(horizontal: 20) means left and right only.

Troubleshooting

4 mistakes you'll probably make today

Cannot provide both a color and a decorationMove color inside BoxDecoration.
Invalid constant value.Remove the const in front of a widget that has changing values.
Undefined name 'colors'.Capital C: Colors.blue, not colors.blue.
Expected to find ','.Each property ends with a comma: title: …,
Tip: put the cursor on a widget and press Ctrl + . to wrap it in a Center, Container or another widget.
Mobile App Development · Lecture 04

The color + decoration error appears as a red error screen when the app runs, not as a red underline in the editor. Show both kinds of errors.

On a Mac the quick-fix shortcut is Cmd + .
